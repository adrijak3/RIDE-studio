How it works

Clear stages, fewer surprises.

  1. Discovery. We discuss needs, scope and budget.
  2. First draft. For template-based designs, usually 1–3 business days after materials and scope are confirmed.
  3. Refinement. One substantial redesign is included; another is typically €30–50 depending on scope.
  4. Launch. After approval I build and test, followed by an agreed support window.

The 1–3 day estimate covers the first visual draft only, not a finished site or booking system.

Testing & support

Support after launch.

One-week testing window after handover to try agreed features and report issues.

Two weeks of technical support — I investigate and fix technical issues with agreed functionality at no charge.

Later maintenance, client-caused changes and new features are quoted separately.
